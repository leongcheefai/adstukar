## How it works

{project.name} plays a channel on your screen. A ticker moves along the bottom of the picture. Each band on the ticker shows one brand.

You earn money while the ticker plays on your screen. A brand pays to be on the ticker. You earn {rate} for each 1,000 brand views, for a maximum of {economy.caps.paidHoursPerDay} hours a day. Refer to the [Wallet](/help/wallet) topic for the full rules.

{project.name} operates in a browser. No installation is necessary. A laptop, a monitor, or a tablet gives the best result.

## Log in

1. Open the app.
2. Select **Sign in with Google**. As an alternative, enter your email address and your password.
3. If you do not have an account, select the link to make one. An account is free.

## Select a channel

After you log in, the app shows the channels. A channel is the picture that plays behind the ticker.

- **Images/Video** plays your own images and videos.
- **Wallpaper** plays a picture that you select from a list.

Select a channel to start it. You can change the channel at any time.

## The dashboard

To open the dashboard, select your account at the top of the channel. Then select **Dashboard**.

The dashboard has four pages.

- **Overview** shows your balance and your recent activity.
- **Campaigns** shows the ticker and your slots. Use this page to book a slot.
- **Wallet** shows each movement of money. Use this page to add funds and to get a payout.
- **Settings** contains your profile and your account data.
