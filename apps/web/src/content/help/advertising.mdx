## What a slot is

The ticker has {economy.slot.count} slots. One slot shows one brand: a logo, a name, and a tagline.

The price of one slot is {usdCents(economy.slot.priceUsdCents)} for {economy.slot.termDays} days. The price is the same for each slot. There is no monthly fee.

## Before you book

Make sure that your wallet has sufficient funds for the slot. To add funds, refer to the **Wallet** topic.

## Book a slot

1. Open the **Campaigns** page in the dashboard.
2. Select an open band on the ticker. This band is your position.
3. Enter the address of your site. The form does a check of the site. Then the form adds the name and the logo.
4. Write a name of {economy.slot.nameMaxLength} characters maximum.
5. Write a tagline of {economy.slot.taglineMaxLength} characters maximum.
6. Add a logo. Use a PNG, JPEG, or WebP image of 5 MB maximum.
7. Confirm the booking.

## Verify your domain

The form tells you to show that you own the site. You can do this step later. But the ad does not play until you complete this step.

## Review

A person does a review of each ad before it plays. The review makes sure that the site is live and that the tagline is accurate.

## Change a slot

You can change the name, the tagline, and the logo at any time. A changed ad gets a new review. The slot and its term do not change.
