import { distributorPercent, economy, playRateRange } from "@repo/config/economy";
import { perThousandPlaysRange, usd, usdCents } from "@repo/config/money";

export const rates = playRateRange();
export const countryName = new Intl.DisplayNames(["en"], { type: "region", style: "short" });

The wallet contains US dollars. The **Wallet** page in the dashboard shows each movement of money on its own line.

## Add funds

You add funds to pay for a slot on the ticker.

1. Open the **Wallet** page in the dashboard.
2. Select the button that adds funds.
3. Enter an amount from {usdCents(economy.topup.amount.minCents)} to {usdCents(economy.topup.amount.maxCents)}.
4. Make the payment on the Stripe page that opens.

The funds show on the **Wallet** page a short time after the payment. No amount gives a bonus. Funds that you added do not expire.

## Refunds

You can get a refund for funds that you added and did not use. Send your request to support in {economy.topup.refundWindowDays} days or less after the payment.

## How you earn money

You earn money when the ticker plays ads on your screen.

- Each play of an ad earns money. The rate is {perThousandPlaysRange(rates.lowest, rates.highest)}.
- When a person scans an ad on your screen, you earn a bonus.
- You keep {distributorPercent()}% of each play and each scan. {economy.feePercent}% is the fee.
- Earned money stays pending for {economy.settlementDelayHours} hours. Then it becomes settled.
- Earned money expires {economy.expiryMonths} months after it becomes settled.

## Get a payout

A payout sends your earned money to your bank account. Only earned money can become a payout. Funds that you added cannot become a payout.

### Conditions

- The minimum payout is {usd(economy.payout.minimum)}.
- Settled money stays on hold for {economy.payout.holdDays} days. Then you can withdraw it.
- Your bank account must be in a country that Stripe can pay.

### Countries

You can get a payout to a bank account in one of these countries: {economy.payout.countries.map((code) => countryName.of(code)).join(", ")}.

### Set up Stripe

Stripe is the company that sends the money. You do this setup one time.

1. Open the **Wallet** page in the dashboard.
2. Select the button that starts a payout.
3. Select the country of your bank account. You cannot change the country later.
4. Select **Connect Stripe**.
5. Complete the steps on the Stripe page. Stripe asks for your name, your address, and your bank data.
6. Wait while Stripe does a check of your data. This check usually takes a few minutes.

### Request the payout

1. Open the **Wallet** page in the dashboard.
2. Select **Request payout**. One request takes all the money that you can withdraw.

A person does a review of each request. Then Stripe sends the money to your bank account. You can make the next request after the last payout is paid.
