## 1. What this policy covers

This policy covers money you add to your CapyChannel wallet (a **top-up**) and the price of a **slot** you book with it. It forms part of the **Terms of Service**.

Money you **earn** from your screens is not refunded. It leaves your wallet only as a payout, under Section 6 of the Terms of Service.

---

## 2. Top-ups

You add money to your wallet in US dollars, any amount from **{topupMinimum}** to **{topupMaximum}**. No top-up carries a bonus. Money you add does not expire.

### 2.1 What can be refunded

You can ask for a refund of the **unspent** part of a top-up within **{refundWindowDays} days** of the payment.

- **Unspent** means money still in your wallet. When you spend, the oldest money you added goes first, so a refund comes from your most recent top-ups first.
- A top-up that is fully spent cannot be refunded.
- After {refundWindowDays} days, a top-up cannot be refunded, but the money stays in your wallet and never expires.

### 2.2 How much comes back

The card processor keeps a fee on every payment (**{processorFee}** at the time of writing) and does not return it when we refund. A refund therefore returns the unspent amount **less that fee**. A refund of part of a top-up carries its share of the fixed part of the fee.

If the unspent amount is smaller than the fee, nothing can be returned.

### 2.3 Currency

We charge in **US dollars** and refund in US dollars. If your card is in another currency, your bank converts the payment and the refund at its own rates, and the two may differ. We do not cover that difference.

## 3. Slots

A slot costs **{slotPrice}** and is paid from your wallet when you book.

- If you remove the ad **before it is approved**, or our review **does not approve** it, the full price goes back to your **wallet** (not to your card).
- Once the slot has **started**, its price is not refunded if you remove the ad, or if we remove it because it breaks the Ads Policy. The slot has already held a place on every screen.
- If we remove a started ad for a reason that is **not your fault** (for example, a complaint about an ad that follows the Ads Policy, a change in the law, or a fault on our side), we return the price of the **unused days** of the term to your **wallet**.
- A slot cannot be paused.

Money a slot returns to your wallet is treated like the rest of your wallet: a card refund of it follows Section 2.

---

## 4. How to ask for a refund

Email **support@capychannel.site** from the address on your account. Name the top-up (its date and amount). An administrator reviews each request; there is no self-service refund button.

We aim to answer within **5 business days**. This is a target, not a promise.

---

## 5. How long it takes

When we approve a refund, the amount leaves your wallet at once and we send the refund to the card you paid with through our payment processor, Stripe. Your bank usually shows it within **5 to 10 business days**. The timing after we send it depends on your bank.

---

## 6. What is not refunded

- Money you earned from your screens (see Section 6 of the Terms of Service). It expires {expiryMonths} months after it settles and may be withdrawn after a {holdDays}-day hold.
- Promotional or granted credit, if we ever issue any.
- The part of a payment the card processor keeps (Section 2.2).
- The price of a slot that has started, except the unused days when we remove an ad that is not at fault (Section 3).

---

## 7. Chargebacks

Please ask us for a refund before you dispute a payment with your bank. If you start a **chargeback** or a payment dispute:

- we may **suspend** your account while the dispute is open;
- we remove the disputed amount from your **wallet**, and a slot that this money paid for may end;
- if the dispute closes in our favour, we restore the amount to your wallet and lift the suspension, unless the account breaks the Terms of Service for another reason.

If the disputed amount is larger than the balance in your wallet, you owe us the difference.
