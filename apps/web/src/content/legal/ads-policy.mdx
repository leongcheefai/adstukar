## 1. Purpose and scope

This Ads Policy sets the rules for listings and campaigns on **CapyChannel**. Advertisers submit **listings** that play on the screens Distributors run in places such as cafes, restaurants, salons, gyms, clinics, shops, and offices. Praxor may approve, reject, or remove listings and campaigns at any time to protect viewers, Distributors, and the integrity of the network.

This Policy applies together with the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

**What a listing is:** you do not upload a designed ad. You give a **name**, a **tagline** of up to {taglineMaxLength} characters, an optional **logo**, and the **website** it links to. The CapyChannel set draws the listing in a band of the crawl along the foot of the screen, and the band links to your website. **[TBD: the bands carry no scan code today (docs/adr/0016); counsel to decide whether the scan sections of this policy stay for a later code.]** The band shows the first {bandNameLength} characters of the name and the first {bandTaglineLength} of the tagline, so put the key words first.

## 3. Required listing standards

Every listing **must**:

1. **Identify a real company or product** — Use the real brand/product name. Do not use placeholder or fake brand names to trick viewers.  
2. **Include a short, accurate slogan or value proposition (tagline)** — Claims must match what the destination actually offers.  
3. **Use English for the name and tagline** — Other languages on the website are fine. **[TBD: confirm English-only for listings shown in Malaysian venues, or allow Bahasa Malaysia and other languages.]**  
4. **Use an HTTPS destination URL** that the Advertiser controls or is authorized to promote; the landing page must load without forcing an unexpected download or install.  
5. **Promote a credible product or service** — Not a fabricated or nonsensical offer.  
6. **Be consistent with the destination** — The name, tagline, and logo must match the website.  
7. **Make supportable claims** — Performance, pricing, health, financial, or results claims must be truthful and supportable.  
8. **Use a logo that is yours**, if you add one — a readable brand mark you own or may use, as a **{logoTypes}** image of up to **{logoMaxMb} MB**. A still image works best; the band shows it small.

## 5. The logo and the code

The CapyChannel set draws every listing the same way, so there is no audio, video, animation, script, or tracking pixel in a listing, and none can be added.

- **The logo** must not contain a scannable code, a website address other than yours, or anything that imitates a screen's own content or a system message (for example a clock, a weather reading, a warning, or a news headline);  
- **A code**, where one is shown with a listing, is made by us. A scan passes through our servers, which count it, and then opens your website. You may not ask viewers to scan a different code.

## 6. Destination (landing page) rules

Landing pages must:

- Use **HTTPS**;  
- Load in a normal browser without drive-by downloads;  
- Not phish or harvest credentials under false pretenses;  
- Not auto-redirect through abusive chains solely to inflate metrics;  
- Remain substantially consistent with the approved listing during the slot — material bait-and-switch may result in the removal of the ad. If we remove an ad for this after its slot has started, the slot's price is not refunded.

## 10. Measurement

A slot is a flat price for a fixed term. There is no charge per play, per scan, or per visit.

A play is counted when a screen reports that the listing crossed it in full while the page was visible, and a scan when a viewer scans the listing's code. The Terms of Service (Section 5) set out how plays are counted. We report plays and scans to advertisers as totals. We do not promise any number of plays, screens, scans, or visits.
