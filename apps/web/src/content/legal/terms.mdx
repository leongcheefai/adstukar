## 5. Measurement and reporting

**What a play is.** A **play** is one approved listing crossing an approved screen in full, while the page is visible, and reported to us by the CapyChannel set. Several listings may be on a screen at the same time. We measure plays from those reports. A play is not proof that a person saw it.

**When a report counts.** A report must reach us while the play is still open: **{livePlayMinutes} minutes** for a play the screen asked for live, or up to **{cachedPlayHours} hours** for a play from the batch a screen keeps for when its network drops. A play whose report arrives later is closed and counts for nothing. A screen reporting late gives the time the play ran; we limit that time to the life of the play.

**When a play earns.** A counted play earns the rate in Section 6 only if, when it is reported:

- the screen is approved;
- it is among the first **{dailyPlayCap}** plays counted on that screen that day (UTC);
- it falls within **{paidHours} hours** of the screen's first play that day (UTC);
- the advertiser's slot is running, and the listing and its campaign are still approved, active, and verified; and
- the Distributor has not vetoed the listing, or excluded a term it contains, on that screen.

A play that fails any of these still counts as shown, and earns nothing.

**Scans.** When a viewer scans the code on a listing, we record the scan and send the viewer to the advertiser's site. A scan earns nothing and costs nothing. We use scans, among other signals, to review payouts (Section 6).

**Our records.** Plays, scans, and earnings are measured by our systems, and our records decide them, except where they are shown to be wrong. **[TBD: counsel to confirm the wording of this clause and a window for disputing a record.]**

**No guarantee.** We do not promise any number of plays, screens, scans, or visits to an advertiser, or any level of earnings to a Distributor.

## 7. Advertisers — campaigns, payments, and billing

### 7.1 Campaigns

An Advertiser creates a **campaign** for one website, proves it controls the website's domain, and adds up to {listingsPerCampaign} **listings**. A person reviews every listing against the **Ads Policy** before it plays. We may approve, reject, or remove a campaign or a listing. An Advertiser may edit or remove a listing; a booked slot cannot be paused.

**Removal is not instant on every screen.** A screen keeps a batch of plays so that it can continue when its network drops. A listing that is removed or rejected is sent to no screen again, but a screen that already holds it may show it for up to **{cachedPlayHours} hours**.

### 7.2 Payments, billing, and refunds

**Top-ups.** You pay by adding money to your wallet (a **top-up**) in US dollars, any amount from **{topupMinimum}** to **{topupMaximum}**, by card through Stripe. No top-up carries a bonus. Money you add does not expire, and it cannot be withdrawn as a payout.

**Slots.** One slot costs **{slotPrice}**. The price comes out of your wallet when you book. The slot's term starts when your campaign's domain is verified and your listing is approved, and it runs to its end; a slot cannot be paused. There is no charge per play or per scan.

**Refunds.** The unspent part of a top-up can be refunded within **{refundWindowDays} days** of the payment, less what the card processor kept. The price of a slot goes back to your wallet if you remove the ad before it is approved or it is not approved; once the slot has started, its price is not refunded.
