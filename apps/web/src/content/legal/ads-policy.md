## 1. Purpose and scope

Native Mac menu-bar apps, dedicated native display clients, and other native apps are **not** part of v1 and are not promised in this Policy.

## 3. Required creative standards

Every creative **must**:

1. **Identify a real company or product** — Use the real brand/product name. Do not use placeholder or fake brand names to trick viewers.  
2. **Include a short, accurate slogan or value proposition (tagline)** — Claims must match what the destination actually offers.  
3. **Use English for the title (company / product name as shown) and tagline** — Title and tagline text on the creative must be in **English**. Other languages on the destination page are fine if the creative’s title and tagline stay English.  
4. **Use an HTTPS destination URL** that the Advertiser controls or is authorized to promote; the landing page must load without forcing an unexpected download or install.  
5. **Promote a credible product or service** — Not a fabricated or nonsensical offer.  
6. **Be consistent with the destination** — Creative text, imagery, and brand must match the landing page.  
7. **Make supportable claims** — Performance, pricing, health, financial, or results claims must be truthful and supportable.  
8. **Include a readable logo or clear brand mark** where the format expects one (image ads).  
9. **Use accepted file formats and size limits** for image creatives: **PNG, JPEG, or WebP under 1MB** (static only), or **text-only** creatives where the product supports them.  
   - Exact dimensions / safe zones: **[TBD: creative specs per surface]** (v1 = web/browser surfaces).
10. **Use static creatives only** — No animated formats (see Section 5).
