## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Praxor Studio (“**Praxor**,” “**we**,” “**us**”) collects, uses, discloses, and retains information in connection with **CapyChannel**, an ad network for screens in places such as cafes, restaurants, salons, gyms, clinics, shops, and offices. The Service includes our websites, the dashboard, the **CapyChannel set** on screens, and our APIs (the “**Service**”).


It applies to:

- **Advertisers** who create accounts, run campaigns, and book slots;  
- **Distributors** who register screens and run the CapyChannel set on them;  
- Visitors to our marketing or product sites — primary domain: **capychannel.site**.

**How the Service runs:** everything runs in a web browser. Members use the dashboard on the website, and a screen runs the CapyChannel set, the same web page, in the screen's browser. We do not offer an app-store app.

This Policy should be read with our Terms of Service and Ads Policy. People in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom should also read Section 13. California residents should also read Section 14. Section 15 explains how to make a data request.

## 2. What we collect

### 2.1 Account and profile information

Depending on your role, we may collect:

- Your name and email address. We do not ask for a phone number or a business name;  
- Your password, if you sign in with one. We store only a one-way hash of it, never the password itself;  
- A profile photo, if you add one;  
- If you sign in with Google: the name, email address, and profile photo that Google shares with us;  
- We do not ask for business registration or tax identifiers in the product today. Stripe may collect them for a payout account (Section 2.5).

### 2.2 Screens (Distributors)

When a screen runs the CapyChannel set, we collect:

- The access key issued for the screen, which the set sends with every request;  
- Each play: which listing played, on which screen, and when;  
- The time the screen last reported;  
- The network a screen reports from, stored as a network prefix instead of a full IP address (the first three parts of an IPv4 address, or the first four groups of an IPv6 address). A prefix can still point to a single home or venue, so we treat it as personal data.

We do not store the screen's browser type or operating system.

When you register a screen for review, we also collect:

- The screen’s name, venue type, and location, as precise as you type it. We need enough to find the venue when we review the screen; we do not need a unit number;  
- A photo of the screen in place, which we use to review the screen. Please avoid including people in the photo. The photo is stored in our file storage at a public link that cannot be guessed. Only the administrators who review screens see the link, but anyone who has it can open the photo;  
- The venue’s stated open hours and time zone;  
- Your own promotion (name, tagline, link, logo), your excluded terms, and the listings you veto.

### 2.3 What we do **NOT** collect by default (important)

Unless we later enable a feature that you clearly opt into and we update this Policy, the Service does **not** collect:

- **Anything about the people in front of a screen.** The CapyChannel set has no camera, no microphone, and no audience measurement. It asks for the listings, shows them, and reports each one that crossed the screen;  
- **Screenshots or recordings** of a screen, a device, or anything else shown on it;  
- **Keystrokes, passwords typed into other apps or sites, or clipboard contents**;  
- **Contacts, photos, or files**, other than the logos you upload as an Advertiser, the pictures and videos you upload to your library in the dashboard, the screen photo you upload for review (Section 2.2), and an optional profile photo;  
- **Full browsing history** outside the Service.

If a future feature changes this, we update this Policy and tell you before the feature collects anything (Section 16).

### 2.4 Advertiser campaign data

- The campaign's name, website address, and verified domain;  
- Each listing's logo, name, and tagline;  
- Pictures and videos you upload to your library in the dashboard;  
- Slot bookings;  
- Review notes and moderation outcomes.

### 2.5 Payments and payouts

**Payments.** You pay for a top-up on a checkout page run by **Stripe**. Your card details go to Stripe, not to us. We keep a record of each top-up: the amount, the time it was paid, Stripe's references for the checkout and the payment, and any refund.

**Payouts.** To be paid, a Distributor opens a Stripe account in their own name through Stripe's hosted onboarding (available in {payoutCountries}). Stripe collects the identity, address, bank account, and any tax details it needs, and holds them under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your bank account number. We keep the Stripe account's ID and country, whether you finished onboarding, and whether Stripe has enabled payouts. For each payout we keep the amount, its value in {payoutCurrency}, the exchange rate and its date, Stripe's reference for the transfer, and the outcome of our review.

**Your wallet.** We keep a record of every movement of money in your wallet: top-ups, slot charges and their refunds, earnings from plays, payouts, and expiries.

### 2.6 Usage, logs, and support

- For each signed-in session: the IP address and browser user agent, and when the session started and ends;  
- Server request logs (the address requested, the time, and the result). Our own logs do not hold your IP address; our hosting provider's logs may hold it. Both are kept for up to 30 days;  
- Support tickets, feedback you send through the dashboard, and correspondence;  
- Cookies and similar technologies (see Section 5).

### 2.7 Aggregated / de-identified data

We produce totals that do not identify you, such as the number of paid plays across the network and the number of screens online, which the website shows.

**Legal bases:** For people in the EEA and the UK, Section 13.1 lists the legal basis for each purpose. For Malaysia, we process your personal data with your consent, which you give when you create an account or register a screen, or where the PDPA allows us to process it without consent, for example to perform our contract with you or to comply with the law.

## 4. Ads targeting and reporting

- **No targeting of people.** A slot plays on every approved screen in the network. The only filters are the ones each Distributor sets on their own screens (excluded terms and vetoed listings). We do not build profiles of viewers or members, and we do not sell personal data.  
- **Reporting:** Advertisers see **daily totals** of their plays. They do not see which screens played their listings or who owns them, unless we must share that to investigate fraud or under legal process. Distributors see daily totals of the plays on their screens, and the listings that may play there, so that they can veto one.

## 5. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only the cookies and browser storage the Service needs to work: a sign-in cookie, and browser storage for your settings in the dashboard and on each screen. We use no analytics or advertising cookies. Our **Cookie Policy** lists them.

We do not show a cookie banner, because we use no cookie that is not strictly necessary. You can control cookies in your browser settings; you cannot sign in without the sign-in cookie.

## 7. Retention

We retain information for as long as needed for the purposes above, including:

- **Account data** (your profile, settings, and uploads): while your account is open, and **90 days** after it closes;  
- **Money records** (top-ups, slot charges, earnings, payouts, refunds, and the plays they rest on): **7 years**, because Malaysian tax law requires it;  
- **Server logs:** up to **30 days**;  
- **Screen photos:** until **90 days** after the screen is archived. The review records of a screen are money records when the screen earned;  
- Listings and logos: as long as the campaign is open, then as account data. A listing that a slot paid for is part of the money records.

We then delete or de-identify when no longer needed, subject to legal holds.

### 13.2 Your rights under the GDPR

Subject to the conditions and exceptions in the GDPR, you have the right to:

- **Access** — confirm whether we process your personal data, and get a copy of it (Art. 15);  
- **Rectification** — have inaccurate personal data corrected, and incomplete data completed (Art. 16);  
- **Erasure** — have your personal data deleted, for example when we no longer need it, unless we must keep it, such as to comply with a legal obligation (Art. 17);

- **Objection** — object to processing based on legitimate interests, and object at any time to direct marketing (Art. 21);  
- **Withdraw consent** — at any time, where processing is based on consent (Art. 7(3));  
- **Complain** — lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live or work, or where you believe the infringement took place (Art. 77). In the UK, this is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

**Automated decisions:** We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you (Art. 22). A person reviews listings and screens before approval, and a person decides every payout request.

## 15. How to make a data request

Anyone can make a privacy request, including a request under the PDPA, the GDPR, or the CCPA.

1. **Send your request** by email to **support@capychannel.site** with the subject line “Privacy request”. Say what you want (for example: access, a copy of your data, correction, deletion, or objection), and give the email address of your account, if you have one.
2. **We verify your identity** before we act, usually by confirming the request from the email address on your account. If you have no account, we may ask for details that let us match your request to data we hold. We use those details only to verify and answer your request.
3. **We respond** within the time the law that applies to you requires: see Section 13.4 for GDPR requests and Section 14.5 for California requests. For PDPA requests, we respond within **21 days**, as the PDPA requires.
4. **If we cannot fully act** on your request — for example, because the law requires us to keep certain records (Section 7) — we will explain why, unless the law prevents us.

We do not charge for a request.
