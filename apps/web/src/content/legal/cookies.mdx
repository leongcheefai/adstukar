## 1. What this policy covers

A **cookie** is a small file a website stores in your browser. **Browser storage** (local storage) is a similar place in your browser where a website keeps data between visits. This policy lists both, for our website, the dashboard, and the **CapyChannel set** on a screen. It forms part of our **Privacy Policy**.

---

## 2. The short version

We use only what the Service needs to work. We use **no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies**, and no third-party cookies on our own pages.

---

## 3. Cookies we set

| Cookie | What it does | How long it lasts |
|---|---|---|
| `better-auth.session_token` (in production, `__Secure-better-auth.session_token`) | Keeps you signed in to the dashboard. It holds a random session token, and your browser sends it only to our servers over HTTPS. | 7 days, renewed while you use the dashboard; deleted when you sign out |
| Sign-in state cookies set by our sign-in system | Protect a sign-in with Google from being forged, while you go to Google and back. | A few minutes |

These cookies are **strictly necessary**: you cannot sign in without them.

---

## 4. Browser storage we use

We keep some data in your browser's local storage instead of on our servers. It stays on that device, and we do not use it to track you.

**In the dashboard:**

- which introduction tips you have already seen;
- the list of pictures and videos in your library, and the date you agreed to the upload terms;
- the last channel you watched, so the dashboard can resume it;
- the screen you were on in this tab, in session storage, which your browser clears when you close the tab.

**On a screen registered to earn:**

- the screen's access key, so it stays registered after a restart, and the channel it last played;
- the batch of listings it plays when its network drops, and the reports it has not sent yet;

You can delete this data by clearing your browser's site data. A screen then needs to be registered again.

---

## 5. Other websites

- **Payments.** When you top up, you pay on a checkout page run by **Stripe**, on Stripe's own website. Stripe sets its own cookies there, for security and fraud prevention, under Stripe's cookie policy.
- **Google sign-in.** If you sign in with Google, Google's own pages set Google's cookies.
- **Advertisers' websites.** A code you scan on a screen opens the advertiser's website, which has its own cookies and policies.

---

## 6. Your choices

You can block or delete cookies and browser storage in your browser's settings. If you block the sign-in cookie, you cannot sign in to the dashboard. Blocking cookies does not affect the public website.

Because we use only strictly necessary cookies and storage, we do not show a cookie banner. **[TBD: counsel to confirm that no consent banner is needed under the PDPA, and under the ePrivacy rules and the GDPR for visitors from the EEA and the UK.]**
